We started 2022 focused on cost (FinOps), stability (Data Contracts), and consolidation (Metrics Layer). We achieved those goals. Our platform is leaner and more reliable than ever.

But the last six weeks have rendered our 2023 plan obsolete.

## The Shift to Vector

We have zero vector databases in production. We have zero embedding pipelines. We have zero prompt engineering guidelines.

We are scrambling to catch up.

## The Reliability Tax

The other big theme of 2022 was "Data Trust." By implementing Contracts and Observability, we finally stopped the bleeding of trust. People believe the dashboards again.

This foundation is critical. You cannot build AI on top of shaky data. If the LLM is trained on garbage, it will produce confident garbage.

## Looking to 2023

2023 will be the year of **Productionizing LLMs**.

We need to move from "Chatting with the bot" to "Building systems that use the bot." That means:
*   **Eval Frameworks:** How do we measure if the bot is getting better or worse?
*   **Vector Search:** How do we give the bot memory?
*   **Cost Control:** How do we prevent a $50k OpenAI bill?

The experimental phase is over. The engineering phase begins now.
