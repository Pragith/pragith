Three years ago, seeing a computer write a poem was magic.
Today, it is a utility.

In 2025, we stopped treating "AI" as a special category.
*   We don't have an "AI Team" anymore. We just have an "Engineering Team."
*   We don't have an "AI Budget." It's just "Compute Budget."
*   We don't have "Prompt Engineers." We just have "Engineers who write English."

## The Normalization

The most exciting thing about 2025 was how unexciting it was. We shipped features. They worked. They used LLMs. Nobody gasped.

We have reached the "slope of enlightenment." The technology is demystified, the costs are understood, and the capabilities are baked in.

## Looking to 2026

The next frontier is **Physics**. Simulating the real world, not just the text world. Robotics and spatial intelligence are waking up.
But for now, I'm just happy that my pager didn't go off this year.
