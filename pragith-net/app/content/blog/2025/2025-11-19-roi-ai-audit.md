The honeymoon is officially over. The CFO asked: "Show me the money."

We tracked three metrics:
1.  **Support Ticket Deflection:** Our RAG bot now handles 45% of Level 1 tickets. Savings: \$1.2M/year.
2.  **Coding Velocity:** Pull Request cycle time dropped by 30%. Engineering output increased without headcount growth. Value: Estimated \$4M/year in avoided hiring.
3.  **Document Processing:** The Multimodal Invoice pipeline processes 50k documents/month. Savings: \$500k/year in BPO costs.

## The Verdict

The ROI is positive, but it wasn't instant. It took 18 months of infrastructure build-out (Vector DBs, Gateways, Evaluators) to reach the break-even point.

AI is not a lottery ticket. It is a capital expenditure with a J-curve return.
