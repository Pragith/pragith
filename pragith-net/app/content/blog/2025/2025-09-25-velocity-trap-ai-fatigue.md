Every week there is a new model. Every month there is a new paradigm.

"Switch to RAG." "No, switch to Long Context." "No, switch to Agents." "No, switch to Edge."

My team is exhausted. The cognitive load of constantly rewriting the platform to chase the state-of-the-art is leading to burnout.

## The Boring Weeks

We have instituted a new policy: **The Boring Week**.
One week per month, we are allowed to touch zero AI code.
*   Refactor the CI pipeline.
*   Update documentation.
*   Fix old tech debt.
*   Take a long lunch.

We need to remind ourselves that we are building software for a business, not competing in a Kaggle competition. Stability is a feature. Rest is a prerequisite for performance.

## What I Would Normalize

I would normalize saying "no" to model upgrades. If a new model does not unlock a real business outcome, it does not get adopted.

We also track on-call load and context switching as first-class metrics. Velocity without sustainability is a short-term illusion.
