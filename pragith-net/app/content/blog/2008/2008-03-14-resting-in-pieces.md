With the past few days being very tiring in college, after coming home, I have not been budging from the corner of my bed.

Though only I have myself to blame for tiring myself out, what can I do? Forget Tuesdays and Wednesdays, when I have my labs  -  the other days, I get lured by seeing my friends playing cricket, and I spring into action as if I have been coiled (like a spring of course) for a long time, and now it's my time to BOING!!! Surprisingly, I have improved at cricket, both batting and bowling.

I thought only I was the one in the lot feeling so tired from doing so little work, but a few of my friends  -  whom I don't wish to name (else the next 3–4 years would be very risky for me)  -  are also suffering the same way.

I don't know what the days ahead have in store for me, but one thing is for sure  -  with the mercury rising steadily in Bangalore, I am going to get more and more tired than ever before.
