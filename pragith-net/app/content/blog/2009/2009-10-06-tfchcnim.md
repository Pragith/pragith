I'm back on Twitter, thanks to [TweetDeck](http://tweetdeck.com/)! It's an Adobe AIR application that helps you post on multiple Twitter accounts and your Facebook account.

And that brings us to **Facebook**. I must confess that I was very much intimidated by its interface long back, and had deactivated my account. But TweetDeck helped me overcome this, and now I'm even more "social" (though I still loathe the stupid quizzes). A few of my school and college friends have also joined Facebook, and are using their "REAL" names (unlike Orkut).

And that brings us to **College**. I'm currently in the middle of 5th semester, and expecting the main exams to get over by 24th December 2009. Also hoping for campus placements this semester or the next one.

No, that doesn't lead us to **[CCNA](http://www.cisco.com/web/learning/le3/le2/le0/le9/learning_certification_type_home.html)**, but I'm going to talk about it anyway. I started studying for CCNA a couple of weeks ago. I've finished ICND 1 and am halfway through ICND 2. It's really, really interesting  -  I even created my own lab with 2 routers and 1 cloud.

I've configured the "Akatsuki" router properly, and it can connect, ping, and traceroute to the internet. I used Microsoft's LoopBack adapter and shared the internet connection from the wireless LAN.

You might have noticed the weird names for the routers, and that brings us to the next topic  -  **[Naruto](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naruto)**. I'm currently hooked on an anime (that's "cartoon" for those who don't understand different forms of animation) called "Naruto". I started with the anime and liked it so much that I even started reading its manga ("comics" in Japanese).

I'm also engaged in a project-making competition organized by IBM known as **[The Great Mind Challenge](http://tgmc.in)** (TGMC). It's mainly a Java-based project involving project planning, modeling, coding, and documenting the software. Me, along with 3 of my classmates, are building an Online Library Management System under the team name  -  **BlueShadow** (cool, huh?).

And the last topic for this post. My friend Vikram introduced me to the "Mozilla Campus Representative" program from Mozilla. It's about promoting and bringing awareness about "The Open Web" and **[Firefox](http://getfirefox.com/)** in your college. Mozilla gives us something known as a "Swag Pack" to help with this.

So, that's the end of my first ever, fully linked, highly informative update. See you after a year or so. I'm so damn tired!
