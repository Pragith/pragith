2023 was "Can we do it?"
2024 was "Can we afford it?"

We spent this year optimizing everything.
*   **Inference:** vLLM cut our GPU bill by 60%.
*   **Training:** LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) allowed us to fine-tune 70B models on consumer hardware.
*   **Caching:** Prompt Caching cut our RAG costs by 90%.

## The "Good Enough" Era

We stopped chasing the "Smartest Model." We started chasing the "Smallest Model that Works."
For 90% of our categorization tasks, a quantized Llama-3-8B is indistinguishable from GPT-4, but runs for pennies.

## Looking to 2025

The frontier is shifting to **Agents that Work**. Not the flaky loops of 2023, but robust, state-machine driven agents that can execute transactions.
We are also keeping an eye on **Video**. Understanding video content is the next multimodal frontier.

See you in 2025.
