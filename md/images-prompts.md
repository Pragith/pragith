Role: Senior Brand Identity Designer
Project: Corporate Service Card for AI Automation Firm
Design System: Dubai S1 – Ultra Professional, High Trust, Minimalist

Canvas:
– 1:1 square format
– Flat off-white background #fafaf9
– No gradients
– No shadows
– No 3D
– No textures

Color Rules:
– All text, icons, lines: #033c89 (deep navy)
– Accent use limited to 5% of canvas: #f29111
– Borders or subtle dividers only: #d4d4d4

Typography Simulation:
– Heading style: Space Grotesk Bold (geometric, modern, professional)
– Body style: Outfit Regular (clean, corporate)

– Uniform thin-to-medium line strokes
– Realistic human proportions
– Professional attire only
– Minimalist tech outlines
– No mascots
– No robots
– No anime
– No cute style
– No glowing holograms
– No futuristic neon
– No isometric

Composition:
– Clean whitespace
– Balanced grid alignment
– Corporate SaaS aesthetic
– Executive-level presentation
